Good questions

Before you drive down

Are the comics new or used?

Both — the shop stocks new and used comic books, and the same spirit covers the rest of the inventory: some brand new, some lived-in.

What decades do you focus on?

The '80s, '90s and early 2000s. That's where the comics, tapes, records and toys come from.

Do you sell art and books too?

Alongside the comics, records and movies you'll find toys and other vintage items. Owner Jasey Treibel told the Longview News-Journal there is no store like hers within 100 miles for art, books and comics.

Where exactly is it?

813 E Methvin St, Longview, TX 75601 — in the historic Junction area of downtown. The shop shares the space with Jensing's Resale & Thrift, run by Jasey's mother, so one trip covers both.

When are you open?

Tuesday through Saturday, 10 a.m. to 7 p.m. Closed Sunday and Monday.
