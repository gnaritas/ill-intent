Mother-daughter run

Two shops, one downtown space

Jasey Treibel and her mother Jennifer Beddingfield opened Ill x Intent Vintage in the historic Junction area of downtown Longview. It shares its space with Jensing's Resale & Thrift — run by Jasey's mother — so one trip covers resale and vintage media in one stop. The shop identifies as women-owned on Google Maps.
