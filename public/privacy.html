Privacy policy

Short and plain — this site collects as little as possible.

What the contact form collects

When you use the contact form, you give us your name, and optionally your phone and email, plus your message. That information is used for one thing only: getting back to you about the shop — browsing, selling, trading or donating.

What this site does not do

No advertising networks. No analytics tracking. No cookies are set by this site itself. No JavaScript runs on your device beyond what your browser does on its own. We don't sell your information — there's nothing to sell.

The one exception: the map

The home and contact pages show a Google Maps embed so you can find us at 813 E Methvin St. When that map loads, Google may set its own cookies under its own privacy policy — this site doesn't control or see them.
