Sell, trade or donate your vintage haul

Inherited a record collection? Emptied the garage and found the boxes of tapes? Ill x Intent Vintage in downtown Longview buys items, makes trades and accepts donations — comics, records, VHS and DVD movies, toys and other vintage goods.

Three ways to pass it on

Cash, trade or a good deed

The shop keeps its doors open on deals of every kind: it buys, it trades, and it takes donations. Pick whichever fits your boxes.

Sell

We buy items

Bring comics, records, movies, toys or vintage goods to the counter at 813 E Methvin St during open hours — or call ahead about a big collection.

Trade

Swap it for shelf finds

The shop trades, so your stacks of comics or tapes can become someone else's — and somebody else's can become yours.

Donate

Drop-offs welcome

Donations are accepted too. Boxes of books, tapes, records or toys can go straight from your garage to the next collector in Longview.

Good questions

Before you haul the boxes

Do you buy items or just take donations?

Both. Ill x Intent accepts donations, makes trades, and buys items — your call which works best for what you've got.

What can I bring?

Comic books, records, VHS and DVD movies, toys and other vintage items. The shop leans toward the '80s, '90s and early 2000s, so items from that era are the sweet spot.

How do I start?

Come by during open hours — Tuesday through Saturday, 10 a.m. to 7 p.m. — or call (903) 704-9393 first to make sure the shop is a fit before you load up.

Can I donate instead of selling?

Yes — donations are accepted at the counter, same as anything else you bring in.

Do you travel to pick up large collections?

Everything happens at the counter on Methvin Street. For a big collection, call (903) 704-9393 and ask.
