What we carry — and what we buy

Ill x Intent Vintage is a vintage and thrift media store at 813 E Methvin St in downtown Longview, Texas. Everything happens in person, on the shelves — open Tuesday through Saturday. Two ways in: dig for your next find, or turn your own attic treasures into cash, trade credit or a good deed.

Call the shop See both services

Service 01

Browse comics, records, VHS & toys

New and used comic books, vintage VHS and DVD movies, records, toys and other items — with a heavy focus on the '80s, '90s and early 2000s.

What you'll find on the shelves

  • New and used comic books
  • Vintage VHS and DVD movies
  • Records to flip through
  • Toys and retro collectibles
  • Other odds, ends and vintage items

Service 02

Sell, trade or donate yours

Ill x Intent accepts donations, makes trades, and buys items. If you've inherited a record collection or emptied a closet full of tapes, bring them downtown.

How it works

  • Bring comics, records, movies, toys or vintage items during open hours (Tue–Sat, 10 a.m.–7 p.m.)
  • Not sure it fits? Call (903) 704-9393 before you haul it over
  • Items the shop takes on go to their next collector
